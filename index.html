Development roadmap

From experiment to experience.

A phased development strategy. This is a creative vision and technical exploration—not a finished game or a funding commitment.

PHASE I · CURRENT

Professor PoE

Build a personal AI research and build-validation tool. Learn to investigate complex skill systems and verify outcomes.

The living story

Expand into a polished single-character fantasy RPG, with deep progression and narrative paths shaped by your build.

The studio & the approach

An ambitious world.
A new way to build it.

Hammer Space Studios is the game-development identity within Applied Heuristics. Our vision is to use advanced agentic AI workflows, specialized research agents, and tool-driven validation to help a small creative team design deeper skill systems, richer worldbuilding, and more reactive stories.

The aim isn't to replace creative direction with automation. It's to combine human authorship with AI-assisted research, iteration, content production, and systematic testing—then prove the approach through increasingly capable prototypes.
